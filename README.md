<h3>The Paradigm Shift</h3>

Modern AI is hitting the "Data Wall" — over 80% of internet data is synthetic or recycled digital noise. DAXSI-Explorer breaks this bottleneck. We transition AI from cloud-bound text mimicry to autonomous physical exploration, giving it a kinetic body to discover, catalog, and verify ground-truth empirical data.
